Joining & Leaving

From Joining Day to Final Settlement

The app handles the whole stay — the day they join, every room change in between, and the day they leave with their deposit settled.

New tenant fills their own details

Show them your PG's QR code. They scan it, fill in their name, phone, address and ID on their own phone, and they appear in your tenant list. No typing for you.

Self Check-in
Allot a bed and collect the first month

Pick any free bed. The first bill is raised straight away with the rent, AC charges and security deposit shown as separate lines, so the tenant sees exactly what they are paying for.

Deposit collected once
Room change, handled fairly

Moving to another bed of the same sharing type happens right away and the rent stays the same. Moving to a different sharing type is scheduled for the start of their next rent month — so nobody is charged twice in one month.

No mid-month surprises
Checkout with the deposit settled

On checkout the app shows any dues left to collect and offers to refund the deposit you are holding. Refunds are recorded as money going out, so your accounts stay correct. The bed turns green immediately.

Deposit refund recorded
If they come back, nothing is lost

Old tenants are hidden from your list, never erased. When a former tenant rejoins, just enter their phone number — their old profile, invoices and payment history come straight back.

Owner & Staff Access

Hand Over a PG Without Handing Over Everything

Own four PGs but only manage one yourself? Give each manager a login that opens only their own property. You keep the full picture.

You, the Owner

Full access to everything

  • All your PGs in one login
  • Rent, payments and profit
  • Expenses and staff salaries
  • Create and remove manager logins
  • All reports

Property Manager

Only the PG you assign them

  • Add tenants and allot beds
  • Collect rent and send reminders
  • Handle complaints and notices
  • Cannot see your other PGs
  • Cannot create logins

Tenant

Only their own details

  • Their rent and due date
  • Their payment receipts
  • Raise complaints, read notices
  • Cannot see other tenants
  • Cannot see your accounts
